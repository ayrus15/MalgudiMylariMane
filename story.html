Our Story

Old Mysore memory, retold through bold nati flavours.

Malgudi Mylari is imagined as a warm heritage house where regional non-veg cooking is treated with both authenticity and premium care.

Where the aroma arrives before the memory does.

The concept draws from Karnataka home kitchens, old military hotel confidence, and the cultural warmth of Mysore hospitality. It is not about generic South Indian food. It is about food with a point of view: country chicken gravies, pepper-heavy mutton, roasted masalas, and dishes that feel lived-in.

The design language intentionally balances luxury and familiarity. Creamy surfaces, maroon depth, and gold detailing let the cuisine feel elevated without disconnecting from its roots.
