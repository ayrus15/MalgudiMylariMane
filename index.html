Our Heritage Story

Born in the royal city of Mysore, Malgudi Mylari represents decades of culinary perfection. Our secret lies in the meticulous preparation of our unique dosa batter and the rich, complex flavors of our traditional chutneys.

Every dish we serve is a testament to the timeless recipes passed down through generations, ensuring that every bite transports you to the nostalgic streets of old Mysore, where hospitality and taste reigned supreme.
